# 12. Email Header Analyzer

## Problem
Investigate a suspicious email by reading what its headers reveal.

## What you will build
- Parse a raw .eml file or pasted headers.
- Trace the Received hops and sending servers.
- Check SPF, DKIM, and DMARC results.
- Flag spoofing signs and mismatched domains.

## Concepts practiced
- Email structure
- Header parsing
- Phishing signals

## Libraries
- email
- re

## Stretch goals
- Resolve and geolocate the sending IPs.
- Score the email's overall risk.

## Authorized use
